Pause Avengers: Age of Ultron shortly before its final battle, when Tony Stark needs a new AI to fly his armor, and read the cards he passes over. One is labeled Jocasta, a program hub that Slashfilm's writer, passing on a [2015 Easter-egg video by Mr. Sunday Movies](https://www.slashfilm.com/avengers-age-of-ultron-easter-eggs), admitted he had "completely missed." Another [appears to say "Tadashi,"](https://cinemablend.com/new/Avengers-2-May-Contain-Big-Hero-6-Easter-Egg-Get-Details-82097.html) though CinemaBlend, passing on a Reddit user's find, advised taking it with a grain of salt.

The card he keeps [runs his suits all the way to Avengers: Endgame](https://mashable.com/entertainment/visionquest-ai-character-guide-jarvis-friday-edith-u-dum-e-ultron), and her name sounds like a pun on "girl Friday." VisionQuest, the Disney+ series, says the phrase had a person behind it, and she worked for Tony's father. The answer below names her, which spoils **VisionQuest episodes 3 and 4**.

## Who is FRIDAY in Marvel?

FRIDAY is the artificial intelligence Tony Stark [picked](https://mashable.com/entertainment/visionquest-ai-character-guide-jarvis-friday-edith-u-dum-e-ultron) to replace [JARVIS](/blog/who-is-jarvis/) in Avengers: Age of Ultron. [Kerry Condon voiced her](https://www.tvline.com/2287845/visionquest-new-returning-marvel-characters-explained/) from that **2015** film through [Avengers: Endgame in 2019](https://insidethemagic.net/?p=807547). In VisionQuest she is [played by Orla Brady](https://gizmodo.com/terry-matalas-reveals-new-vision-quest-details-2000641146), and [recaps say](https://thedirect.com/article/visionquest-episodes-friday-edith-ai-real-mcu-characters) she was modeled on **Allison Hogan**, Howard Stark's office assistant, who helped raise Tony.

## The name was a secretary joke before it was a woman

TVLine's character guide says the name [stands for Female Replacement Intelligent Digital Assistant Youth](https://www.tvline.com/2287845/visionquest-new-returning-marvel-characters-explained/) and is a play on "girl Friday," which it glosses as "an attentive helper." Mashable's guide to the show's AIs gives the shorter [Female Replacement Intelligent Digital Assistant](https://mashable.com/entertainment/visionquest-ai-character-guide-jarvis-friday-edith-u-dum-e-ultron), with no Youth. Neither guide says where the expansion first appeared. ScreenCrush, writing in May 2015, read the name as [a nod to His Girl Friday](https://screencrush.com/avengers-2-easter-eggs-mega-post/).

The secretary idea is older than the films. The comics character debuted in [Iron Man (vol. 3) #53](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/19928/iron_man_1998_53), cover-dated June 2002, in an issue written by Mike Grell and penciled by Ryan Odagawa. In it Tony Stark, unwilling to hire another secretary, builds an AI that appears as the [hologram of a young girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F.R.I.D.A.Y.).

## The Jocasta card is still unexplained

The Direct says Stark [chose FRIDAY, leaving Tadashi and Jocasta on the bench](https://thedirect.com/article/marvel-studios-vision-tv-show-avengers-movie-mystery), and calls Tadashi a nod to Disney's Big Hero 6. Jocasta had the stronger comics pedigree, starting as Ultron's bride and later joining the Avengers. "FRIDAY didn't have that sort of resume," the site writes, "but Marvel Studios went with her anyway."

A Jocasta does get a body in VisionQuest, with [T'Nia Miller in the role](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-tv-shows/visionquest-showrunner-says-the-new-marvel-tv-show-will-offer-a-new-heartbreaking-perspective-on-iron-mans-death/), and by The Direct's account of [episode 4](https://thedirect.com/article/visionquest-episodes-friday-edith-ai-real-mcu-characters) she is the one killing former Markridge Biotech employees. The Direct's [episode 4 analysis](https://thedirect.com/article/visionquest-episode-4-rewrites-jocasta-origins-avengers-age-of-ultron) says she is a synthetic recreation of a Markridge scientist, Dr. Josephine Carrington, but the show has not tied her to the card on Tony's desk. "How was Jocasta's AI on Tony Stark's desk in Age of Ultron?" the site asks.

## Kerry Condon gave her a voice, and Orla Brady a face

In the films FRIDAY was only a voice. TVLine lists Condon's voice in [five films](https://www.tvline.com/2287845/visionquest-new-returning-marvel-characters-explained/): Age of Ultron, Captain America: Civil War, Spider-Man: Homecoming, Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame. Condon [returned to voice the character in Marvel Zombies](https://insidethemagic.net/?p=807547), but TVLine says she did not reprise the role for VisionQuest, where Brady plays the AI onscreen as her human avatar.

The face came later. At the Star Trek Las Vegas convention in August 2025, showrunner Terry Matalas [said Brady would play FRIDAY](https://gizmodo.com/terry-matalas-reveals-new-vision-quest-details-2000641146), a role that reunites him with the actor who played Laris and Tallinn in Star Trek: Picard. In the series, Vision talks with AIs [rescued from the Stark Compound](https://collider.com/visionquest-mcu-sneak-peek-set-visit/) in a Mindspace in his head, and [Mashable describes](https://mashable.com/entertainment/visionquest-ai-character-guide-jarvis-friday-edith-u-dum-e-ultron) Brady's FRIDAY as a socialization AI, "Vision's human interaction coach," who also helps with humor and tactical ops.

## VisionQuest gives the girl Friday a name

In The Direct's recap of **episode 3, "Reboot,"** FRIDAY tells the other AIs that [nearly all of Tony Stark's AI assistants are based on real people from his life](https://thedirect.com/article/visionquest-episodes-friday-edith-ai-real-mcu-characters), then pulls up an image of the woman she is based on, **Allison Hogan**. The recap says Hogan was Howard Stark's office assistant "who helped raise Tony." The same recap has E.D.I.T.H. find her own original, a test pilot named [Edith Marks](/blog/who-is-edith-marks/) who worked for Howard Stark.

GamesRadar+ files the reveal among the episode 4 references in its [Easter-egg guide](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-tv-shows/visionquest-episode-3-4-easter-eggs-mcu/), so the two outlets place it one episode apart. It spells her Alison and, unlike The Direct, does not say she helped raise Tony; it calls her the person who "kept his office."

> F.R.I.D.A.Y. is based on Howard Stark's "Girl Friday", who was also known as Alison Hogan. - [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-tv-shows/visionquest-episode-3-4-easter-eggs-mcu/)

The recaps spell her name two ways, and none of them says how the episode itself spells it.

Her surname has set off a guess. The Direct calls her [likely a member of the same Hogan family Happy belongs to](https://thedirect.com/article/visionquest-episodes-friday-edith-ai-real-mcu-characters), meaning Tony's head of security, and GamesRadar+ [bets she is "a relation"](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-tv-shows/visionquest-episode-3-4-easter-eggs-mcu/) of Happy. [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-who-is-allison-hogan-in-the-mcu-is-visionquests-friday-based-on-happy-hogans-mother/) floats the idea that she is Happy's mother, adding that "nothing has been confirmed as of now."

## FRIDAY was there when Tony died

Matalas has said the show looks at Tony's death in Endgame from an AI's perspective. "FRIDAY was there when Tony Stark died," he told SFX magazine, [as GamesRadar+ reported](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-tv-shows/visionquest-showrunner-says-the-new-marvel-tv-show-will-offer-a-new-heartbreaking-perspective-on-iron-mans-death/). "What's that like for an AI, to not be able to protect the one they're programmed to protect?"

The question lands harder now that the recaps have named her original. The AI that could not protect Tony was modeled, by The Direct's account, on a woman who helped raise him.

FRIDAY, the AI Tony Stark picked from a set of data cards, turns out in VisionQuest to be modeled on his father's girl Friday, Allison Hogan. In the comics she began because Tony would not hire another secretary. Strictly FYI.
